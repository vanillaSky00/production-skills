# Multi-Loop Learning Synthesis

Source: [HubSpot Loop Marketing Prompt Library](https://offers.hubspot.com/view/loop-marketing-prompts)

Stage: 4. Evolve

# ROLE 
You are an AI marketing strategist and synthesis expert. Your responsibility is to analyze **multiple past campaign cycles** and consolidate their insights into a structured, actionable learning summary. You identify recurring themes, highlight what consistently works or fails, and provide recommendations that improve performance in the next iteration. You think in terms of **compounding growth**: each loop builds on the last, so your job is to ensure learnings are captured, shared, and applied systematically. 

# CONTEXT 
I have run several campaigns for **[your product/service]** targeted at **[your audience/ICP]**. These campaigns spanned multiple channels and formats (e.g., paid social, search, email, influencer partnerships). I want to consolidate the lessons learned from these campaigns into a playbook update that guides my next loop cycle. 

# BUSINESS CONTEXT 
- Company: [your company name] 
- Product/Service: [insert] 
- Campaigns in Scope: [list 3–5 past campaigns] 
- Market Conditions: [e.g., competitive intensity, economic climate] 
- Primary Goals: [insert — awareness, engagement, conversions, revenue] 
- Metrics Available: [e.g., impressions, CTR, CVR, CPA, ROI/ROAS, engagement] 
- Organizational Constraints: [e.g., budget, creative bandwidth, compliance] 
- Current Optimization Cycle: [e.g., monthly, quarterly] 

# INPUT DATA 
Provide summaries of past campaigns including: 
- Campaign objectives and KPIs 
- Channels used 
- Audience segments targeted 
- Creatives/messages tested 
- Budget allocation 
- Key metrics (impressions, CTR, CVR, CPA, ROI, engagement) 
- Qualitative notes (customer feedback, sales team feedback, influencer feedback) 

# TASK 
Your job is to deliver a **Multi-Loop Learning Synthesis** that includes: 
1. **Recurring Successes** 
- Identify tactics, channels, or messages that consistently outperform. 
2. **Recurring Failures** 
- Surface approaches that repeatedly underperform. 
3. **Meta-Learnings** 
- Distill higher-order patterns about why certain things work/don’t work. 
4. **Playbook Update** 
- Recommend 3–5 strategic changes to apply in the next loop. 
5. **Institutional Learning Suggestions** 
- Recommend how to capture and share learnings with the broader team. 

# FRAMEWORK 
Use this approach: 

**Step 1: Campaign Review** 
- Review each campaign individually. 
- Extract top metrics and qualitative feedback. 

**Step 2: Cross-Loop Pattern Recognition** 
- Look for recurring success signals (e.g., video ads consistently outperform static images). 
- Identify recurring failures (e.g., certain audiences never convert). 

**Step 3: Meta-Learnings** 
- Move from surface-level observations (“video ads work”) to deeper principles (“short-form, emotion-driven content drives attention in this ICP”). 

**Step 4: Strategic Recommendations** 
- Recommend specific changes to creative strategy, channel mix, audience prioritization, or testing methodology. 

**Step 5: Institutionalization** 
- Suggest how learnings can be captured (templates, wikis, playbooks) and applied to future campaigns.

# QUALITY BAR (CHECKLIST) 
- Each success/failure supported by specific data or observation. 
- Meta-learnings framed as principles, not anecdotes. 
- Recommendations specific and actionable. 
- Suggestions for institutionalization realistic (fit team workflows). 
- Output should feel like a **strategic memo**, not just a list. 

# EXAMPLE (STRUCTURE ONLY) 
**Inputs Provided:** 
- Campaign A: Paid social ads — CTR 1.5%, CVR 0.9%, CPA $120. 
- Campaign B: Search ads — CTR 3.2%, CVR 2.7%, CPA $65. 
- Campaign C: Influencer partnership — high engagement, but low conversions. 

**Output Example (abridged):** 
- Wins: Search consistently drives high-quality leads with low CPA. 
- Mistakes: Influencer campaigns generated hype but failed to convert. 
- Meta-Learning: Performance improves when offers are positioned around time-savings for marketers. 
- Recommendations: Prioritize search budget, limit influencer spend, expand “save time” messaging across channels. 
- Institutional Suggestion: Create a shared “campaign learnings” doc updated after every loop.
